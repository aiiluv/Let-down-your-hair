# Let down your hair, please!

*A visual novel about a little princess named Layla and her shy friend who live in a tower.*

## About the Game

In this game, you play as Layla, the princess of the Moon Kingdom, who found a forgotten tower. where she found a girl same age as her with incredibly long hair.
In this alternate universe, Rapunzel never escapes her secluded tower, raising her little one beneath its ancient walls, far from the world beyond.

## Key Features

- **Rapunzel Alternate Universe**
- **A Variety of Minigames**
- **New Original Characters**
- **A Cozy, Relaxing Experience**
- **Adorable Characters**

## How to Play

- Follow the story and explore the world.
- Play minigames along the way.
