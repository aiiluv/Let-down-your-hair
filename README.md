## About the Game

Layla, the princess of the Moon Kingdom, stumbles upon a forgotten tower hidden deep within an enchanted forest, where an ethereal maiden with hair spun from golden sunlight lives in solitude with her child.

In this alternate universe, Rapunzel never escapes her secluded tower, raising her little one beneath its ancient walls, far from the world beyond.

## Key Features

- **Rapunzel Alternate Universe** — A world where she never leaves her tower.
- **A Variety of Minigames** — Lots of fun little minigames to play.
- **New Original Characters** — Discover the story through the eyes of new characters.
- **A Cozy, Relaxing Experience** — Take your time and enjoy the story at your own pace.
- **Adorable Characters** — Meet charming, lovable characters.
